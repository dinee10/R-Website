Related work covers plant image recognition, herbal knowledge retrieval, cultivation support and explainable AI. The individual proposals contain the detailed reviews.

Research gap

Existing approaches often treat these areas separately and provide limited Sri Lankan context or interpretable AI results.

Research problem

How can AI improve access to medicinal plant identification, knowledge, cultivation advice and quality information in Sri Lanka?

Objectives

Investigate four connected AI methods to support medicinal plant recognition, knowledge access, cultivation decisions and quality analysis.

Research components
