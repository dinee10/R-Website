Literature survey

Previous research uses deep learning for plant recognition, knowledge bases and chatbots for herbal information, and machine learning for crop recommendations. Explainable AI methods such as Grad-CAM help show which image regions influence quality predictions.

Read the individual proposals →

Research gap

Existing approaches often address identification, herbal knowledge, cultivation and plant quality separately. The research focuses on Sri Lankan medicinal plants, Sinhala and English knowledge access, and interpretable AI results.

Research problem

Medicinal plants can be difficult to identify, reliable traditional knowledge is not always easy to access, and small-scale farmers need better information about cultivation suitability and market conditions.

Research objectives

Explore an integrated AI-based decision-support approach for plant identification, bilingual herbal knowledge, cultivation recommendations and explainable plant quality assessment.

Research components

01 · IT22168986

Plant Identification

Identify Sri Lankan medicinal plants from leaf and flower images using computer vision and deep learning.

02 · IT22640598

Herbal Knowledge

Answer Sinhala and English questions about medicinal plants through retrieval-augmented generation (RAG).

03 · IT22132796

Cultivation Recommendation

Recommend suitable herbs using soil, climate, market demand and estimated economic outcomes.

04 · IT22112132

Quality Assessment

Assess leaf condition and maturity, with Grad-CAM explanations of image-based predictions.

Methodology

The proposal outlines literature review, dataset preparation, component development, system integration and evaluation. Each component uses methods suited to its task, including image classification, knowledge retrieval and cultivation analysis.

Technologies

The proposal includes Python, mobile development, computer vision, machine learning, RAG and Grad-CAM. The later research paper reports EfficientNetV2-B0 for quality assessment and ChromaDB with language-model embeddings for herbal knowledge retrieval.

Reported findings

The supplied research paper reports the following results for the quality-assessment and conversational-assistant components:
